Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Commit & Push (C&P) is a training app made by Sebastian Frøiland, a private person in Norway. He decides how your data is used and is responsible for it (the data controller under the GDPR). Contact: commitandpushapp@gmail.com

What we collect

  • Your email address and a user id, from Sign in with Apple or the code we email you, to keep your account and your data together.
  • Your training log: workouts, sets, weights, reps, how a set felt, notes, skipped exercises, goals, injuries and preferences, to save your training and let the coach plan your next workout.
  • Morning check-ins: weight, sleep and energy, to fit the plan to how you feel.
  • Coach chat: your messages and the photos you add, so the coach can answer you.
  • Spoken messages to the coach, turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. Only the text is saved, never the recording.
  • Apple Health numbers: heart rate, sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, breathing, steps, active energy and date of birth, only after you allow it in iOS.
  • Feedback you send about the app, to fix bugs and improve it.

We use no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites and no analytics services, and we never sell your data.

Health data

Health numbers are read from Apple Health only after you allow it in iOS. They go to the coach only if you also say yes in the app, and you can change that any time in Settings, under Coach. Health data is never used for advertising and never shared beyond what this policy lists. The legal basis is your explicit consent (GDPR article 9(2)(a)).

Who processes your data

  • Supabase, EU (Stockholm, Sweden): stores your account, training log and photos.
  • Anthropic, USA: runs the AI coach. It reads your training log, chat and photos, and your health numbers if you said yes, to write your plan and answers. What it reads is not used to train its models.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Health on your phone, and speech recognition for spoken messages.
  • GitHub, USA: holds the developer's private notes, where feedback you send about the app may be copied so it can be worked on.

The developer may keep a working copy of your data on his own computer, for example to look into a problem. Such copies are deleted together with your account. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as these companies offer them.

Who can see your data

You can. The AI coach reads it to plan for you. The developer can read stored data to run the service, fix problems and answer you. Nobody else does, unless the law requires it.

Legal basis

  • Your account and the coach: to provide the service you asked for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).
  • Health data: your explicit consent (article 9(2)(a)), which you can take back at any time.
  • Keeping the app secure and working: our legitimate interest (article 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, your account, training log, chat, photos and health numbers are deleted from our database and storage at once. We keep no backups, so nothing is left behind.

Your rights

You can see, download, correct and delete your data. Tap your name in the menu: Export downloads everything, and Delete account deletes everything and cannot be undone. Turn health sharing off in Settings, under Coach, or in the Health app. You can also object to or ask us to limit how we use your data: write to commitandpushapp@gmail.com and we answer within 30 days. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (datatilsynet.no).

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, the age of consent for online services in Norway, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we tell you in the app first. The date at the top shows the latest version.
