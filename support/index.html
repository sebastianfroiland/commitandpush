Support

Commit & Push (C&P) is a training app with an AI coach that reads your log and plans your next workout.

Contact

Write to commitandpushapp@gmail.com. We answer as soon as we can, and always within 30 days.

Your data

  • Export: tap your name in the menu, then Export. It downloads everything you have saved.
  • Delete account: tap your name in the menu, then Delete account, and tap again to confirm. Everything is deleted at once and cannot be undone.
  • Health sharing: turn it off in Settings, under Coach, or in the Health app.
